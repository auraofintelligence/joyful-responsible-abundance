The cost of shared computing

How large is the proposed investment?

The Australian plan starts with 3,000 sites and an initial allowance of A$6 million per site. Multiplying those gives A$18 billion, within the stated A$18–19 billion planning range. The global comparison uses 44,000 sites at the same allowance, giving A$264 billion.

Initial capital estimate

A$18 billion

3,000 sites × A$6 million per site

The allowance covers an initial installation and support package. Running costs, staff, electricity, maintenance and replacement need continuing budgets. These are planning scenarios, and actual site costs need engineering and procurement work.

A scale you can compare

What does a gigawatt mean over a year?

Power is the rate at which electricity is used. Energy is the amount used over time. Think of a tap and a bucket: the flow rate is like power, while the water collected is like energy. This calculator makes the sovereignty paper’s electricity comparison visible.

Annual electricity use

113.88 TWh per year

About 39.7% of the paper’s 286.8 TWh Australian comparison.

The formula is gigawatts × 8,760 hours × utilisation ÷ 1,000. The fixed comparison is Australia’s 2025 generation of 286.8 terawatt-hours as reported in the paper. This measures electricity, not useful computing or intelligence. Actual loads and annual output vary.
